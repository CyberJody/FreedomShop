Migrate from QuickBooks POS

Upload the Items, Customers and Vendors files from the Export utility / Export to Excel. Departments come from items. Use CSV (comma or tab), TXT/TSV, or XLSX; text may be UTF-8 or Windows cp1252. Save older XLS files as XLSX or CSV first. Maximum 20 MB and 100,000 data rows per file.

Every upload gets a dry run before Import is available. Review unknown columns, duplicate rows, department mappings and tax categories. Possible duplicates are skipped for manual review.

The optional Sales History report becomes read-only receipts. The report must have receipt number, date, item, quantity, price, line extension and line tax. Tender labels alone do not supply payment amounts: those receipts import totals only, and payment-based reports will be incomplete.

Backups (.qpb), open orders, deposits, account balances, gift balances and purchase history are not supported by this export path.
